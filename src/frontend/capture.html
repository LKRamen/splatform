Capture Mode — Gaussian Splat Instructions

Follow these steps to film Fordham Lincoln Center for tomorrow's live splat integration.

What to Capture

  • Film the main ground-floor lobby and plaza of Fordham Lincoln Center — the spaces the robot will traverse. Aim for a ~20×20 m capture zone.

  • Walk in slow, overlapping arcs. Never walk backwards. Keep the phone held at chest height and pointed slightly downward (15–20°). Speed: about 0.3 m/s (very slow).

  • Cover the scene from three distinct heights: ground-level (0.5 m), chest-level (1.2 m), and overhead (2 m, phone raised). 2–3 loops at each height.

  • Avoid fast pans, motion blur, or direct sunlight reflections on glass. Shoot in overcast light or interior lighting — consistent exposure is key.

  • Capture close-up detail passes for any furniture, pillars, or landmarks the robot will navigate around — 1 m distance, slow 360° orbit.

Minimum frames: 300–500 photos for a room-scale capture. Postshot will auto-extract from video — film at least 4 minutes of continuous footage.

Output file: save as scene.splat and drop it into assets/. The server serves it at /assets/scene.splat and the viewer loads it automatically on next refresh.

Luma AI (Free Tier Alternative)

  • Upload video to lumalabs.ai — free tier supports up to 5 min video.

  • Once processed, download the Gaussian Splat (.ply) from the export menu.

  • Convert: python scripts/ply_to_splat.py scene.ply assets/scene.splat (uses the gsplat library).

Quality note: Luma free tier limits resolution. PostShot with a local GPU gives noticeably sharper results for architectural interiors.

Integration Checklist

  • Drop scene.splat into assets/

  • Restart server: python -m uvicorn src.backend.server:app --port 8765

  • Refresh the trainer — the splat loads automatically with a progress overlay

  • If splat coordinate system is misaligned, adjust the Y-axis offset in viewer.js → tryLoadSplat()
